Tube Flip

Privacy Policy

Chrome extension · Last updated: 21 August 2026

In short: Tube Flip does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The extension has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no background servers. It does not read, record, or send your browsing history, video history, search queries, or any other information anywhere.

How the extension works

The extension runs only on YouTube watch pages (https://www.youtube.com/watch). It adds rotate and flip buttons to the video player and changes how the current video is displayed by applying a CSS transform to the video element inside your own browser. All processing happens locally on your device. Nothing leaves your computer.

Permissions

The extension requests no special Chrome permissions. Its content script accesses YouTube watch pages solely to insert the player buttons and apply the visual transform described above. This access is not used to collect or transmit any data.

Third parties

The extension does not send data to any third party and does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Because the extension collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
