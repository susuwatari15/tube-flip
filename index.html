Turn any YouTube video the right way up

Tube Flip adds rotate and flip buttons to the YouTube player, so you can fix sideways or mirrored footage in one click — right from the controls bar.

See how it works

Free · No account · Works on youtube.com/watch · Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

Three buttons, right where you need them

New controls appear next to the Settings gear on every watch page.

Rotate 90°

Each click turns the video a quarter-turn clockwise: 0° → 90° → 180° → 270°. Sideways clips are scaled to stay inside the player.

Flip horizontal

Mirror the video left-to-right — perfect for selfie-cam footage or text that came out backwards.

Flip vertical

Mirror top-to-bottom. Rotation and both flips combine freely, so you can get to any orientation.

See it in action

Same player you already know — just a few extra buttons.

Zero data collected

Tube Flip has no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no servers. It only changes how a video is displayed inside your own browser.
